Best Way to Use This App for Analysis
The best way to use this application for your BI portfolio analysis is to treat it as a case study where you apply the tool to a specific stock (or a small set of stocks) and then analyze the output from a business intelligence perspective. Instead of just showing the features, you'll be demonstrating your ability to interpret the data the tool generates and derive actionable insights.
Here's a strategic approach:
1.	Select a Stock Wisely: Don't just pick any stock. Choose one that has some interesting characteristics or recent activity (e.g., a stock that has been volatile, a well-known company, or one in an industry you find interesting). This will give you more to analyze and discuss. You could even pick one that has had a significant event (earnings report, news, etc.) and see how the forecast aligns (or doesn't).

2.	Focus on the BI KPIs: The "Business Intelligence KPIs" section of your app is key. This is where you'll extract the core insights for your report.
3.	Document Everything: Take screenshots of the Streamlit UI at various stages (forecast summary, technical indicators, BI KPIs). This will be crucial for your report.
4.	Tell a Story: Your analysis should tell a story about the stock. What did the tool predict? What were the key drivers of that prediction (e.g., historical trends, seasonality)? How do the technical indicators support or contradict the forecast? What are the risks and opportunities based on the market correlation?
